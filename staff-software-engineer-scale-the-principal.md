# Staff Software Engineer — Architecture & Delivery

**Planet Depos | Remote | $170,000–$200,000**

---

## The Role

We need a Staff Engineer to partner with our Principal Engineer (who owns platform architecture and AI initiatives) to scale engineering output across two teams. This is not an AI specialist role — the Principal already owns AI architecture. This role exists to absorb systems design, spec authorship, code review, and product-to-engineering translation so the Principal can focus on platform-level initiatives.

You will collaborate directly with the Principal to:
- Design service boundaries and maintain architectural diagrams (C4, ERD, sequence)
- Break product requirements into actionable, spec-driven work packages
- Review PRs for adherence to SOLID principles, design patterns, and clean architecture
- Mentor engineers and uphold code quality standards across teams
- Represent engineering in product conversations and technical planning

---

## What You'll Do

**Architecture & Design (25%)**
- Collaborate with the Principal on architecture decisions; draft ADRs for review and own the documentation
- Apply common design patterns (Factory, Strategy, Repository, Adapter, etc.) appropriately
- Maintain the architecture decision record (ADR) catalog for your domains
- Design event-driven service boundaries that enable team autonomy

**Spec Translation & Work Breakdown (25%)**
- Translate product tickets into technical specs with WBS breakdowns
- Define acceptance criteria that engineers can execute against
- Identify cross-team dependencies and sequencing risks

**Code Review & Quality (25%)**
- Review PRs across 1–2 teams for architectural alignment
- Enforce SOLID principles, design patterns, and clean architecture conventions
- Coach engineers on refactoring strategies and pattern application

**Hands-On Development (20%)**
- Own complex or cross-cutting implementations that set patterns
- Build reference implementations for new architectural approaches
- Write architecture fitness functions and automated guardrails

**Technical Leadership (5%)**
- Attend leadership and planning meetings as engineering representative
- Contribute to hiring and interview loops
- Mentor Senior engineers toward Staff-level growth

## Required Qualifications

**You must possess all three. Candidates with only one or two will not be considered.**

### 1) Deep knowledge of design patterns and architectural patterns
You can recognize when to apply Factory vs. Builder, understand the trade-offs between repository and active record, and defend dependency injection over service location. You've shipped systems where pattern discipline enabled testability, maintainability, and team scalability.

**Qualifying experiences include:**
- Systems you designed using layered, hexagonal, or clean architecture
- A pattern glossary or style guide you authored for your team
- Refactors you led that replaced anti-patterns with appropriate design patterns

### 2) Spec-driven development or strong technical writing
You translate ambiguity into structured documents that engineers execute against. You've written specs that answer "what," "why," and "how to verify" before code is started.

**Qualifying experiences include:**
- A spec you authored that reduced implementation churn or rework
- A WBS you created that enabled parallel development across engineers
- Technical documentation you maintain that serves as source-of-truth for your team

### 3) Code review as mentorship
You treat PR review as a teaching opportunity, not a gate. You can explain why a pattern violates SOLID, suggest concrete refactors, and guide engineers toward better designs without taking over their work.

**Qualifying experiences include:**
- Review history showing coaching comments, not just "fix this"
- A style guide or pattern glossary you authored for your team
- Architecture fitness functions or automated checks you implemented to guide code quality
