# Interview Loop Redesign — Meeting Checklist
**Date**: Wednesday  
**Attendees**: You + Senior Developer  
**Goal**: Lock the new interview architecture, assign ownership, schedule pilot

---

## 1. Current State Validation (5 min)
Confirm we're solving the right problem.

- [ ] Senior dev observed same gaps? Any you missed?
- [ ] Specific candidate examples where 1hr wasn't enough?
- [ ] Team capacity: How many devs can realistically rotate? (Start: __ devs)

---

## 2. The New Loop Architecture (15 min)

### Time Allocation
| Stage | Current | Target | Owner |
|-------|---------|--------|-------|
| HR Screen | 30 min | 30 min | Recruiter |
| Technical | 1 hr | **2 hrs** | You + rotating dev |
| Director/VP | 1 hr | **30 min** | Director/VP |
| **Total** | 2.5 hrs | **3 hrs** | — |

- [ ] Director/VP time reduction: Preliminary buy-in? Or needs conversation?

### Technical Stage Breakdown (2 hrs)
| Segment | Duration | Focus |
|---------|----------|-------|
| Warm-up / Work History | 15 min | STAR behavioral questions |
| Coding Challenge | 60 min | Architecture, refactoring, SOLID principles |
| System Design | 30 min | High-level design, trade-offs |
| Candidate Questions | 15 min | Their questions for us |

- [ ] Coding challenge "done" criteria defined? (e.g., "refactor complete, pattern negotiated")
- [ ] System design question bank: Who drafts first 3 questions?
- [ ] STAR rubric: What traits are we screening for?
  - [ ] Ownership
  - [ ] Collaboration
  - [ ] Conflict resolution
  - [ ] Technical judgment

### Round-Table Debrief
- [ ] Timing: Same day? Next day?
- [ ] Format: Sync meeting? Async doc first?
- [ ] Attendees: Who must be there?

## 3. Ownership & Rotation Model (10 min)

### Senior Developer Growth Role
- [ ] Co-own the rubric and question banks?
- [ ] Run first "shadow" interview with new format?
- [ ] Own system design question maintenance?
- [ ] Lead round-table debriefs?

### Rotation Schedule
- [ ] Who rotates in? (List: _______________)
- [ ] Frequency: ___ interviews/month per dev
- [ ] Shadow policy: New interviewers shadow you ___ times before solo

## 4. Stakeholder Alignment Plan (10 min)

### Director/VP
- [ ] Who leads alignment conversation? You or senior dev?
- [ ] When? ___
- [ ] What's the pitch? (Better signal, reduced no-hire regret)

### HR/Recruiter
- [ ] Do they need updated candidate brief?
- [ ] Sourcing criteria change? (e.g., more senior-focused)
- [ ] Scheduling template update for longer technical slot?

### Candidate Experience
- [ ] Expectation-setting: Clearer brief on what technical stage covers?
- [ ] Interviewer intro: "This is a 2-hour collaborative session..."

---

## 5. Pilot Program (10 min)

- [ ] First pilot candidate: ___ (or "next 2 candidates")
- [ ] Pilot timeline: ___

### Success Metrics
- [ ] You feel confident in hire/no-hire decision
- [ ] Candidate feedback positive on process fairness
- [ ] Team feels ownership/engagement in hiring
- [ ] Reduced "maybe" votes (clearer signal)

### Rollback Trigger
If after ___ candidates we're not seeing:
- [ ] Clearer signal quality
- [ ] Positive candidate experience
- [ ] Team engagement

→ Revert to 1hr format and reassess.
