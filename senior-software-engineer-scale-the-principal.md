# Senior Software Engineer — Architecture & Team Enablement

**Planet Depos | Remote | $150,000–$170,000**

---

## The Role

We need a Senior Engineer to join our platform team and help scale engineering output across two teams building event-driven systems in AWS. This role partners with the Principal Engineer and a Senior Engineer (your peer) to translate product requirements into executable work, maintain code quality, and grow toward Staff-level responsibilities.

This is not an AI specialist position. Our Principal owns AI architecture. This role exists to accelerate delivery through solid systems design, spec-driven work breakdown, and rigorous code review — while exposing you to the patterns and practices that define Staff-level engineering.

---

## What You'll Do

**Work Breakdown & Spec Authorship (25%)**
- Translate product tickets into technical specs with clear acceptance criteria
- Break features into actionable work items for the team
- Document dependencies and sequencing for cross-team initiatives

**Code Review & Quality (25%)**
- Review PRs for SOLID principles, design patterns, and clean architecture alignment
- Mentor junior engineers through constructive feedback
- Enforce architectural standards and flag violations early

**Hands-On Development (25%)**
- Implement features in TypeScript (and Java as needed)
- Own medium-complexity services and domain modules
- Apply appropriate design patterns to solve problems

**Systems Design (20%)**
- Contribute to architecture diagrams and documentation
- Design service boundaries within established domain constraints
- Participate in ADR discussions and technical planning; collaborate with the Principal on design decisions

**Growth & Leadership (5%)**
- Lead small initiatives and mentor less experienced engineers
- Contribute to hiring (interview loops, code exercises)
- Absorb Staff-level responsibilities over time as you grow

## Required Qualifications

**You must possess all three. Candidates with only one or two will not be considered.**

### 1) Solid grasp of design patterns and architectural principles
You understand when to use Factory over Builder, why dependency injection beats service location, and how repository patterns isolate domain logic from data access. You've shipped production code where pattern discipline made the codebase more maintainable.

**Qualifying experiences include:**
- Production systems you built applying SOLID principles consistently
- Codebases you've worked in that used layered or clean architecture
- Refactors you led to improve separation of concerns, testability, or pattern consistency

### 2) Product-to-engineering translation
You've taken vague requirements and turned them into technical plans that engineers executed. You ask clarifying questions, identify edge cases, and write tickets/specs that reduce back-and-forth during implementation.

**Qualifying experiences include:**
- Tickets or specs you wrote that engineers implemented with minimal clarification
- Ambiguous requirements you decomposed into actionable work items
- Cross-functional collaboration with product or design that led to better outcomes

### 3) Code review as quality gate and teaching tool
You review code for more than bugs — you look for design patterns, architectural alignment, and opportunities to share knowledge. You can explain *why* a change is requested, not just *what* to change.

**Qualifying experiences include:**
- Review history showing mentorship and pattern guidance
- Style guides or conventions you helped establish on your team
- Times you caught architectural issues in review before they reached production
